# Imagens demonstrativas do hero

Ferramenta: imagegen integrada (sem CLI). Imagens geradas por IA; não são fotografias de serviços do Barbi Lava Car.

Arquivos finais:
- `public/images/hero-photo-after.webp`
- `public/images/hero-photo-before.webp`

As duas imagens têm 1920 × 640 px, WebP qualidade 88. A conversão de formato e redução de resolução foram feitas com Sharp. A versão suja foi gerada por edição da imagem limpa; não são carros diferentes.

## Prompt da versão limpa

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: photorealistic automotive before/after slider clean master image. Generate a genuinely photographic-looking silver modern compact hatchback car, recognizable realistic proportions similar to a Volkswagen Golf, parked in strict side profile facing right on a dry pale concrete floor in a clean light grey car detailing studio. Entire vehicle including both bumpers and all wheels visible, vehicle centered horizontally with 12% margin on both sides. Wide cinematic image aspect ratio 3:1, camera at door-handle height, straight horizon, fixed tripod, 70mm lens appearance. Clean glossy paint, realistic tires and glass, natural softbox reflections. Neutral white/light grey wall, minimal red accent line far in background, no props, no people, no text, no logos, no license plate text, no watermarks. Premium actual automotive photography, absolutely not illustration or 3D rendering. This clean image will be edited into a dirty version; strong stable geometry and simple background are critical.

## Prompt da versão suja (edição da versão limpa)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the edit target, the CLEAN master for an interactive before/after comparison. Create its DIRTY BEFORE version. Change ONLY the surface cleanliness of the car: add very visible realistic road dust, dried muddy splashes on lower doors and wheel arches, dirty wheels, dusty windows, subtle rain dirt streaks on paint. Keep SAME silver hatchback, EXACT pixel alignment, silhouette, wheel positions and spoke orientation, body panels, reflections, crop, camera, floor, background and red line. Absolutely no movement, geometry changes, perspective changes, zoom or reframing. Do not add props, people, text, logos or watermarks. Photorealistic actual car photography with convincing road dirt, not rust, damage or painted marks. Preserve original image dimensions and composition precisely. Every edge must remain stationary compared with the input.
